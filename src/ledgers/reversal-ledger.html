Project 2025 means the item appears in Heritage's Mandate for Leadership or its implementation; legal movement means litigation or legal theory developed by conservative lawyers and groups; industry request means an affected industry asked for it; congressional allies means Republican legislators drove it by statute; Trump's own means the president or his appointees' personal initiative.

"Origin" records who created the thing being undone. "Bipartisan" marks laws passed with support from both parties or policies kept by presidents of both parties. "Longstanding" marks norms and arrangements older than modern party lines.
