How to read the levers

A Democratic Congress cannot fire a defence secretary, and conviction needs 67 senators. What it can do is fund, fence, legislate and ask questions under oath. Five levers matter:

  • Appropriations: funding levels and binding riders in the defence bill, which the president must sign to keep the Pentagon funded.
  • NDAA: the annual defence authorisation, which has passed every year for more than six decades and carries policy the Pentagon must follow.
  • Oversight: hearings, subpoenas, Inspector General and GAO referrals. Needs no signature.
  • Senate: confirmations, holds on nominees and, if it comes to it, an impeachment trial.
  • Courts: cases already running, such as Senator Mark Kelly's suit over his censure.
