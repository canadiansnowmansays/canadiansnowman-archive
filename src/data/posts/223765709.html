PREVIOUSLY, IN THE ADVENTURES OF THE KING…

Long-time readers will remember how it all began. In August 2023, our Hero was summoned to a Washington courtroom to answer for certain events of January 6, 2021, and found waiting for him a federal judge with a law book, a calendar and no apparent interest in being flattered.

The Hero defeated that storyline the classic way, by winning an election, which in this universe makes the case disappear. The Judge returned to her chambers. As every reader of the genre knows, this is precisely what nemeses do between issues. They wait.

Meanwhile, the Hero turned to building things: a ballroom, a name on a performing-arts centre, a gilded Oval Office. And, at last, his masterwork, a 250-foot triumphal arch at Memorial Circle, by the entrance to Arlington National Cemetery.

Our story opens on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

ROGUES’ GALLERY

THE HERO’S ARSENAL

Specifications of THE ARCH, as filed with the Division.

HEIGHT: 250 feet. Washington’s buildings are limited by federal law to 130 feet, as lawyers for the District and Virginia pointed out in court. The Division can confirm that the remaining 120 feet are for the cape.

AIRSPACE: The Federal Aviation Administration has studied whether the arch would interfere with flights in and out of Ronald Reagan National Airport. A hero’s monument should always be visible from the air. Ideally not from the cockpit, at close range.

DEFENCES: Drone swarms. Snipers’ nests. Both are fully integrated into the design since roughly last week.

ORIGIN OF DEFENCES: The Hero’s earlier adventure, THE BALLROOM, in which judges hesitated to interfere with construction on the White House grounds because of national-security claims, and the ballroom rose nearly to completion despite orders to stop. The Division regards the arch as a sequel. The Arch Enemy, regrettably, has read the first issue.
