CLASSIFICATION: UNCLASSIFIED // SUITABLE FOR SIMULTANEOUS PUBLIC AND PRIVATE CONSUMPTION
SUBJECT: Iranian Policy Posture, General Assembly Hall and Environs, 22 September 2026
PREPARED FOR: The Principal, care of Whoever Briefs Him Next

1. PURPOSE

This report exists to reconcile, for internal filing purposes only, two postures adopted by the United States Government toward the Islamic Republic of Iran within the same calendar day, at the same address, involving overlapping personnel. Reconciliation was attempted. Reconciliation was not achieved. Both postures have been filed as simultaneously operative pending further guidance, which the Office does not expect to receive.

2. TIMELINE OF EVENTS

0929 hrs. The Principal takes the podium. Opening remarks establish a baseline of general excellence across all measurable categories of American life. No mention yet of Iran. Office notes this as the calm before.

1044 hrs. Posture Alpha is formally adopted. The Principal informs one hundred and ninety-three member states, assembled specifically to discuss the peaceful resolution of disputes, that he retains sole personal authority to decide between (a) permitting the Islamic Republic to “rebuild and create a far greater country than it ever was before” or (b) annihilating it “quickly, never giving them a chance to kill and destroy people and countries again.” The word “annihilate” is used without a qualifying clause. The Office notes that Article 2(4) of the founding Charter of this same institution technically covers this exact scenario, and that the institution’s own podium was, structurally speaking, not built to host it.

1109 hrs. Posture Alpha is restated with added texture: the Principal notes that the Islamic Republic is “waiting to see how I do in the midterm election,” and predicts a deal will follow the November vote regardless of anything said in the preceding forty minutes. Office logs this as evidence that Posture Alpha may be a negotiating instrument rather than a foreign policy, a distinction this report is not equipped to resolve and will not attempt to.

~1215 hrs (approximate — no official minute-taker present). Posture Beta commences, unannounced, several floors removed from the podium. Special Envoy Witkoff and Senior Adviser Kushner convene with Foreign Minister Araghchi of the just-threatened nation for a duration later described by the Principal as “three hours” and as “very good.” Meeting is mediated by the governments of Qatar and Pakistan, both of whom appear to have anticipated Posture Beta well in advance of its formal adoption. Subject matter: “a pathway to a deal.” No annihilation-related agenda items are recorded.

1315 hrs. The Principal, briefed on Posture Beta approximately one hour after concluding remarks establishing Posture Alpha, characterises the day’s work to reporters as follows: “It’s potential greatness or obliteration.” Office notes this sentence as the single document in which both postures achieve simultaneous, grammatically correct expression, and recommends it for framing.

1505 hrs. Posture Beta acquires specificity. Iranian state television reports the just-threatened foreign minister’s conditions for a deal: lifting of the United States naval blockade on Iran’s southern coast, release of frozen Iranian funds, and cessation of hostilities on all fronts, in exchange for reopening the Strait of Hormuz. Office notes that these are the demands of a government engaging in what appears to be ordinary, if strenuous, diplomacy, rather than a government bracing for the alternative outlined under Posture Alpha, Section 2, above.

1600 hrs. Iran’s armed forces issue a statement describing the Principal’s remarks as “exploitation of the U.N.’s podium to legitimize aggression, insecurity and global banditry” and dismissing Posture Alpha as “baseless.” Office notes that the just-threatened party appears to have correctly identified which posture it was dealing with, despite the Office’s own continuing internal uncertainty on the matter.

Ongoing, all day. Foreign Minister Araghchi is separately observed meeting, in sequence and evidently in good spirits, with the European Union’s foreign policy chief and the foreign ministers of the United Kingdom, Italy, Austria, Qatar, and Azerbaijan, each encounter documented in warm photographs on his own social media account. Office is unable to reconcile a diplomatic schedule of this density with the “isolated pariah state” framing understood to be operative under Posture Alpha, and has downgraded Isolation Status to UNDETERMINED pending further study.

3. ASSESSMENT

Both postures remain, as of this filing, simultaneously true. Posture Alpha was delivered to one hundred and ninety-three nations on live television. Posture Beta was conducted in a room this Office was not invited into. No mechanism currently exists within the Department for determining which posture the public should retain as the operative one, and no such mechanism is anticipated before the next General Assembly session, at which point this report will likely require only minor date changes.

4. ANNEX A — APPROVED TALKING POINTS, EITHER CONTINGENCY

The following language has been cleared for use regardless of which posture proves, in retrospect, to have been the real one: — “The President has been clear and consistent throughout.” — “Nothing said today should be read as inconsistent with anything else said today.” — “We do not comment on the existence or non-existence of parallel diplomatic tracks, several of which may or may not be occurring at this time, possibly in this building.” — “Ask Qatar.”

5. DISTRIBUTION

This report has been filed in the Office’s permanent archive under both P (Posture Alpha) and P (Posture Beta), which the Office notes, without further comment, share a first letter.

End of report. A revised version may follow, depending on the hour.
