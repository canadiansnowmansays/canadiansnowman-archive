Prospero. Fourth quarterly health check. In good health, as always. Wayne said the research had been adequate. This was the first sign.

Wayne Ellerby acquired the ferret in March.

He has not fully explained why. When asked — and he has been asked, by Gerald at the municipal building, by Carly for the Beacon, and by Maggie at the clinic on three separate occasions — he says that it was a considered decision made after what he describes as “adequate research.” This is the only known instance of Wayne Ellerby describing his own research as adequate rather than thorough, comprehensive, or complete, which is itself an indication that the research was not adequate, and which Wayne knows, and which everyone who knows Wayne also knows, and which has been left unaddressed because in Elsinore there are certain admissions that are more useful when they remain implicit.

The ferret’s name is Prospero.

Wayne chose this name before he had fully grasped what a ferret was, during a period of optimism about the arrangement that has since been revised. Prospero is the magician in The Tempest — Alistair’s production of which opened in April to considerable acclaim and a standing ovation that Wayne attended in the Presidential Package complimentary seat he reserves for cultural diplomacy. Wayne felt that naming the ferret after Prospero captured something of the Festival’s spirit and also, in retrospect, something of the ferret’s disposition, which is imperious, unpredictable, and convinced of its own authority over its immediate environment.

The ferret’s quarterly health check is conducted by Maggie at the Perth County Small Animal Clinic in Millbank.

The fourth quarterly check occurred on a Tuesday in November.

Maggie had seen Prospero three times before. She had formed a view of Prospero on the first visit that she had not shared with Wayne, because the view concerned not the ferret’s health but Wayne’s fitness for ferret ownership, and Maggie considers it outside the clinical scope of a veterinary appointment to tell a client that they have made a lifestyle error, even when this is evident.

Prospero was in good health. Prospero was always in good health. Prospero had the robust constitution of a creature that had assessed its environment, found it manageable, and proceeded to manage it with the comprehensive self-assurance that ferrets bring to all engagements with the world they inhabit.

What had changed, by the fourth visit, was Wayne.

He arrived at the clinic with the particular expression of a man who has been managing something and is no longer entirely certain the management is going in the right direction. He set Prospero’s carrier on the examination table. Prospero emerged from the carrier with the brisk efficiency of a creature that considers the carrier a temporary imposition and the examination table an appropriate next step.

Maggie examined Prospero. Prospero submitted to the examination with a quality of conditional tolerance that suggested he was permitting it rather than receiving it.

“He’s fine,” said Maggie.

“Yes,” said Wayne.

“He’s always fine,” said Maggie.

“Yes,” said Wayne.

Maggie looked at Wayne. In fourteen months of quarterly appointments, Wayne had never said only yes. Wayne had asked about optimal ferret enrichment environments, the relative merits of various ferret dietary supplements, the documented impact of ferret ownership on client testimonials in the tourism sector — this last having prompted Maggie to recommend a specific brand of supplement and to note, in Prospero’s file, that the owner’s relationship with the animal had motivational complexity.

“What’s he doing,” said Maggie, “that he wasn’t doing before.”

Wayne said Prospero had begun escaping from his enclosure on a nightly basis, conducting what Wayne described as “unsanctioned assessments” of the apartment, and returning to the enclosure before morning in a manner that suggested he considered the enclosure a base of operations rather than a home.

He said Prospero had reorganised the items on the lower shelf of Wayne’s bookcase. Not disordered them. Reorganised them. Wayne had come home to find his Elsinore tourism brochures arranged by date rather than by format, which was the system Wayne used, and which was, Wayne acknowledged, probably less logical than chronological order, but which was his system.

He said Prospero had developed an interest in Wayne’s client spreadsheets, specifically in sitting on the laptop keyboard during the hours Wayne used for revenue forecasting, which produced, on two occasions, entries in the Presidential Package booking column that were not bookings but were the word gggggggggg repeated across several cells.

Maggie listened to all of this. She examined Prospero, who had settled on the examination table with the air of a witness who has already given his statement and considers the matter closed.

“Wayne,” she said. “The ferret is bored.”

Wayne said he had purchased three enrichment devices recommended by a ferret specialty retailer in Guelph.

Maggie said the ferret was not bored of enrichment devices. The ferret was bored of Wayne’s apartment. The ferret required more varied engagement with its environment than a tourism consultant’s home office provided.

Wayne said he had been considering whether Prospero might benefit from accompanying him on certain professional activities.

Maggie looked at him.

“Not the river tour,” she said.

“The Bieber Heritage Walk,” said Wayne. “Controlled environment. Leash-compatible harness. The clients in Seoul have expressed strong interest in authentic Elsinore experiences.”

Maggie said nothing for a moment. She was thinking about the swans.

“The Swan Warden,” she said, “will have views.”

Wayne said he had not yet consulted the Swan Warden.

“You should consult the Swan Warden,” said Maggie.

Wayne said he would consult the Swan Warden.

He did not consult the Swan Warden.

The incident occurred on a Thursday in late November, during a Gold Level Literary River Tour that Wayne had extended, at the clients’ request, to include the Bieber Heritage Walk.

The clients were a group of four from Seoul — not the original Presidential Package client, but members of the Facebook group he had founded, which now had two thousand and forty members and which Wayne monitored with the professional attention of a man who understands that community management is its own form of tourism infrastructure.

Prospero accompanied the tour in a harness Wayne had researched extensively and which Prospero had accepted with the conditional tolerance he extended to all of Wayne’s arrangements.

The first two waypoints proceeded without incident. Prospero attracted significant photographic attention from the clients, which Wayne had anticipated and which he had incorporated into his revised tour notes under the heading Authentic Elsinore Wildlife Encounter, a description Maggie would later describe, when Carly read it to her over the phone, as “optimistic.”

At the proposed Bieber statue site, where the PROPOSED INSTALLATION SITE marker had achieved its own celebrity following the photograph in which the swans had occupied the bank behind it, Prospero became very still.

Wayne had been explaining the heritage significance of the location. The clients were photographing the marker. Prospero, on his leash, was attending to something Wayne could not immediately identify.

The something was a swan.

Specifically, it was Gertrude — the large female who runs the east bank, who has bitten the AI Swan Management Optimization Report, who has been on the Avon since before the Tourism and Culture Committee existed, and who was at that moment on the bank approximately four metres from the proposed installation site, attending to her own business with the self-possession of a creature that has never in her life been surprised by anything.

Prospero and Gertrude regarded each other for four seconds.

Then Prospero made a sound that Wayne’s tour notes would later describe as “a vocalisation” and that Maggie, when told about it, identified as the sound a ferret makes when it has decided that a situation requires its personal attention.

Gertrude rose to her full height. She was considerably taller than Prospero. She was considerably taller than Wayne. She spread her wings, which she does when she wishes to make a point, and the point she wished to make was that the south bank was her bank and had been her bank before Elsinore Heritage Tours existed and would be her bank after it ceased to exist, and that a ferret on a harness, however authentic an Elsinore wildlife encounter it might represent, was not a variable she was prepared to accommodate.

Wayne picked up Prospero.

The clients photographed everything.

The Swan Warden, who had been conducting her November habitat survey thirty metres upstream and who had noted the group’s approach in her dark green notebook twelve minutes earlier, wrote three words when Gertrude spread her wings.

She did not share what the three words were. She put her notebook away. She walked back to her bench.

Prospero and Gertrude regarded each other for four seconds. Then Prospero made a vocalisation. Gertrude spread her wings. Wayne picked up Prospero. The clients photographed everything. The Swan Warden wrote three words in her notebook and put it away.

Maggie heard about the incident from Jim at Sunday dinner. Jim had heard about it from one of the labrador’s owners, whose daughter followed the Elsinore Facebook page and had seen the photographs, which had been posted by two of the Seoul clients within eleven minutes of the incident and which had received, at last count, six hundred and forty-two reactions.

Jim passed the potatoes. He said he thought Prospero had shown initiative.

Maggie said initiative was one word for it.

He said Gertrude had also shown initiative.

Maggie said Gertrude had shown something closer to institutional authority.

Jim said he thought there was a difference between initiative and institutional authority and that the difference was approximately four seconds and one very specific sound.

They ate their dinner. The boys ate their dinner.

Wayne filed an incident report with the Tourism and Culture Committee, which he described as a “minor operational variance” in the Gold Level experience that he was “in the process of reviewing.” He noted that client satisfaction scores remained high and that the incident photographs had generated significant social media engagement.

Gerald received a copy of the incident report. He filed it under Elsinore Heritage Tours — Prospero — Incident — November 2026 — Swan Engagement.

He noted, in his personal records, that this was the first entry in the Prospero subfolder. He anticipated it would not be the last.

Prospero is in good health. He has been seen, on two subsequent occasions, sitting in the window of Wayne’s apartment on Ontario Street, watching the street below with the focused attention of a creature that has been out in the world and is considering what comes next.

The Swan Warden has not mentioned the incident in her quarterly habitat report. She has noted, under November observations, that Gertrude’s territorial behaviour remains consistent with prior years. She has not elaborated. She does not need to.

A Note for the Record

The ferret is not a political animal.

This is, in the current moment, a distinction worth noting. The political animals of the south have been numerous, loud, and persistent in their territorial assertions, their unsanctioned assessments of environments they have entered without adequate research, their reorganisation of systems that were working, and their production, across various official records, of entries that resemble the work of something sitting on a keyboard.

Prospero reorganised Wayne’s tourism brochures by date rather than format. He considers the enclosure a base of operations. He has conducted nightly unsanctioned assessments of the apartment and returned before morning.

Several agencies of the federal government of the United States have been reorganised in the past year by people who arrived without adequate research, considered their positions bases of operations rather than responsibilities, and conducted assessments that their predecessors, their staff, and in several cases the courts, have described as unsanctioned.

Gertrude spread her wings. She made her point. She went back to her bank.

In Washington, the institutional equivalent of Gertrude spreading her wings has been slower and noisier and has involved considerably more litigation. But the principle is the same: certain things have been here longer than the current arrangement and intend to remain longer still, and have methods of making this clear that do not require a harness or a leash or a Tourism and Culture Committee incident report.

Prospero is in good health.

The swans are proceeding.

Gerald has filed the incident.
