Welcome to my weekly recap of Substack posts that include long-form political analysis, historical musings, a nerdy jump into AI, science and technology, and the occasional satire.

The subject matter is wide but the perspective is consistent: Canadian, outsider, evidence-based, and sardonic in the tradition of Stephen Leacock, who understood that the view from slightly outside the room is often the clearest one available.

The core preoccupation is American politics — its institutions, its corruptions, its capacity for self-renewal, and the consequences it generates for everyone who lives within its considerable gravitational pull, which is most of the world. But the series range from AI governance and energy policy to foreign affairs, Canadian trade, space exploration, consumer advocacy, and the long history of democracies under pressure. Some are analytical. Some are investigative. Some are satire sharp enough to require a label.

A warm welcome to new subscribers. Start with the topic that brought you here, then browse — you’ll find the same voice throughout every series.

Smaller items that don’t need a full essay go into short Dispatches, and the first two appeared this week. Individual essays won’t arrive by email; you’ll find them all here in the Sunday recap.
