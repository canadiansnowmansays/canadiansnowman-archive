A note before the certificate: every measurement in the Historical Record below is real and on the public record. The Office, its stadiometer, its working groups and its methodology are invented. The Office would like it noted that it finds this distinction unhelpful.

THE OFFICE OF RETROACTIVE JUSTIFICATION Division of Vertical Affairs Washington, D.C.

CERTIFICATE OF PRESIDENTIAL STATURE No. 2026-0603

This is to certify that the President of the United States has been measured, re-measured, and where necessary measured again until the correct result was obtained, and that he stands six feet three inches (6′3″), a figure the Office regards as final, historic, and in no way subject to the tape.

Preamble: Findings of the Office

After a decade of polling, focus groups, academic conferences and books with the word “Why” in the title, the Office has at last identified the source of the President’s enduring appeal. He is tall. Political scientists have shown that the taller candidate wins the popular vote about two times in three (see Section 4). The Office regards the matter as closed.

The Office wishes to make clear that it measures height only. It does not measure hands, and it has declined every request to do so since March 2016, when the President raised them on a debate stage in Detroit and personally assured the nation, “I guarantee you there’s no problem.” The Office accepts the President’s guarantee in full, and has nothing further to add about any part of him that is not his height.

(And yes, I was only talking about his hands. — S.G.)

Section 1: Historical Record

The Office has assembled every known official measurement of the President for the purpose of demonstrating their perfect consistency.

Findings. Between the ages of eighteen and sixty-nine, the subject grew one full inch, an achievement unknown to medical science and, the Office believes, worthy of the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, which the Office has also written to.

Between February 2019 and August 2023, the subject lost twenty-eight pounds without diet, exercise or any change of menu, then regained nine of them by April 2025. The Office attributes the August 2023 figure to the well-documented slimming effect of being booked into a county jail in Georgia, and does not recommend it as a programme.

Section 2: Methodology

To ensure that all future measurements produce the correct result, the following procedures are now in effect:

2.1 Calibration. The White House stadiometer has been recalibrated to begin at one inch. Measurements taken on the old instrument will be adjusted retroactively.

2.2 Hair. Presidential hair is classified as load-bearing structure and is included in all measurements. Wind conditions on the day of measurement will be noted for the record and corrected in the subject’s favour.

2.3 Footwear. The subject will be measured in shoes, as he is never seen in public without them and the Office measures only the President the public actually sees.

2.4 Posture. The subject will stand as tall as he stood on the day he was certified, which was very tall, possibly the tallest anyone has ever stood. People said so.

2.5 The Tape. The tape measure will be held by a member of the subject’s own staff. Independent tape-holders have been found to have an agenda.

Section 3: Schedule of Rival Diminution

The Office notes that a President’s height is relative, and has therefore maintained, since 2016, a programme to reduce the stature of others. The programme operates by nickname and has achieved results no stadiometer could.

Note on the method. The Office observes that no rival has ever been designated “Big.” This is policy.

Section 4: Historical Benchmarking

The political science cited in the Preamble, a study of every American presidential election, is in the Office’s view the most important work ever produced by a university, and the only one it has not tried to defund.

The Office has accordingly benchmarked the President against his predecessors:

  • George Washington (about 6′2″). Surpassed by one inch. The Office has prepared a plaque.

  • Thomas Jefferson (about 6′2½″). Surpassed by half an inch. A smaller plaque.

  • Lyndon Johnson (about 6′4″). Exceeds the President by one inch. Under review on the grounds that Texans measure everything larger.

  • Abraham Lincoln (about 6′4″). Exceeds the President by one inch, plus the hat. A Working Group on the Lincoln Question has been convened and has been sitting since 2017. Its interim finding is that the hat should not count, that Lincoln stood in an era before modern measuring standards, and that if Lincoln were alive today he would be the first to say the President is taller.

  • James Madison (about 5′4″). The Office mentions Madison only to cheer itself up.

Section 4A: Forward Planning: The Next Field

At the request of the Director, the Office has begun measuring possible successors, on the principle that the next President should be shorter than the current one and, ideally, should know it.

A note on female candidates. The Office was asked whether women should be included in this schedule. The Office asked whether it really had to. It was told that it did.

The Office notes for the record that height has never yet decided an election on its own, and that the two candidates above were shorter than every man they ran against and still won more votes between them than the Office would like. This finding has been referred to the Working Group on the Lincoln Question, which is now also the Working Group on Several Other Questions.

Section 5: Debate and Public Appearance Provisions

5.1 At any debate, the opponent’s lectern shall be placed in a slight depression, which the Office will describe as “the natural slope of the venue.”

5.2 In any group photograph with foreign leaders, the President shall stand on the step above, or, where there is no step, a step will be provided and later described as having always been there.

5.3 Should the President appear beside a person who is visibly taller, including but not limited to members of the British royal family, professional athletes and the occasional Secret Service agent, the photograph shall be cropped, reissued, or reclassified as an artist’s impression.

5.4 The phrase “looked up to” shall be used in all official communications, as often as grammar allows and frequently when it does not.

Section 6: Certification

The undersigned certifies that the foregoing is true, accurate and final, and that any subsequent measurement producing a different result will be treated as a measurement error, a hoax, or the work of the Radical Left Tape Measure Lobby.

Signed,

Director, Division of Vertical Affairs Office of Retroactive Justification

Countersigned, standing on a box,

Deputy Director (Acting)

APPENDIX A: Doorframe

The Office has also taken custody of an old doorframe, recovered from a kitchen, on which some four hundred rulers have been marked in pencil over roughly two thousand years. Every crown on it has a line beneath it, showing where the ruler actually stood, except one, which appears to have been drawn above the scale by its owner. The Office has certified this mark as accurate. The kitchen’s previous occupant, a 2000 year old man, has declined to comment, except to say that he will be addressing the question of height, and what it is not, on Boxing Day.
