Here is this week’s collection of Substack essays: political analysis at length, a wander or two through history, a deep dive into AI, science and technology, and a satire whenever the news deserves one.

The topics roam widely, but the vantage point stays put: Canadian, a little outside the fence, grounded in evidence, and dry in the manner of Stephen Leacock, who knew that someone standing just beyond the doorway often sees the room most clearly.

American politics sits at the centre of it all: its institutions and their corruption, its stubborn habit of renewing itself, and the fallout for everyone caught in its orbit, which is most of the planet. From there the series branch out into AI governance, energy, foreign affairs, Canada’s trade fight, space, consumer advocacy, and the long record of democracies under strain. Some pieces dig. Some explain. A few are satire, clearly labelled, in case anyone mistakes them for the real thing.

If you’re new here, welcome. Begin with whatever drew you in, then wander. The voice is the same in every series.
