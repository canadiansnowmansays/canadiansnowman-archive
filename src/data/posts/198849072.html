EDITOR’S NOTE: The following is a reconstructed transcript of President Donald J. Trump’s toast at the wedding of Donald Trump Jr. and Bettina Anderson, held Saturday, May 24, 2026, at a private venue in Palm Beach, Florida. The president arrived unannounced, approximately ninety minutes into the reception, as a security precaution. We all remember what happened the last time a president attended a large public event at a Hilton. The president departed before the cake was cut. Also as a security precaution.

Thank you. Thank you very much. Beautiful. What a beautiful — look at this room. Not as big as my ballroom, the new one, which is going to be 90,000 square feet of impenetrable steel and also impenetrable glass, but nice. Very nice. For a — what is this, a tent? It’s a tent. It’s a very nice tent. I’ve seen bigger, but it’s — for a wedding, it’s fine. It’s fine.

I wasn’t going to come. I told Don — I said, “Don, the timing is not great. I have a thing called Iran. You may have heard of it. Big situation. Very dangerous. Everybody says I’m handling it better than any president in history, but it’s a lot of work, a lot of phone calls, a lot of very important decisions that only I can make.” And he understood that. He understood. He’s a good kid. He gets it.

But I also told him — I said, “Don, if I come, I get killed by the fake news. If I don’t come, I also get killed by the fake news. So it’s a lose-lose, which, by the way, is not something I’m used to. I don’t usually lose. I certainly don’t lose twice. But with the fake news, you can’t win. You cannot win.”

So I came. As a surprise. For security reasons, which I can’t get into, but let’s just say the Secret Service wanted me to do it this way. They said, “Sir, it would be better if you arrived unannounced.” And I said, “That’s fine, because frankly, the element of surprise is something I’m very good at. Ask Iran.”

Don — stand up, Don. There he is. Look at him. Big guy. Strong. Not as tall as me, but who is? I remember when he was born. Ivana wanted to name him Donald. I said — and this has been reported, so I can say it — I said, “You can’t do that. What if he’s a loser?” And you know what? So far, so good. Mostly. He’s had some ups and downs. We all know about the ups and downs. But he’s here, he’s — this is number two, right? Wedding number two. So we’re — we’re optimistic. Very optimistic. Hopefully they’re going to have a great marriage. Hopefully.

And the bride — where is she? Stand up. Beautiful. Truly — a very, uh — she’s a person that I’ve known for a long time. Very long time. Palm Beach. We’re all Palm Beach. And she looks — the president is very happy with the way she looks. Very satisfied. I told someone that recently, and they printed it, which is fine because it’s true. She looks fantastic. I don’t remember her name right now because I’ve been dealing with Iran all morning — seven calls, very intense, very classified — but she knows who she is. She’s right there. In the white dress. The beautiful white dress. Not as beautiful as Melania’s, but — no one’s is. That’s just a fact. Even the fake news would admit that.

I want to talk about the venue for a second because I think it’s relevant. This is a nice place. Very nice. But I have to be honest — and I’m always honest, the most honest president, they say — it’s not the White House. Now, if we had done this at the White House, in the new ballroom — which, by the way, is going to be the most incredible room in the history of rooms, 90,000 square feet, the chandelier alone is going to be — you wouldn’t believe the chandelier. But we couldn’t do that because the construction — it’s going beautifully, by the way. Ahead of schedule. Under budget. The steel is impenetrable. The glass is impenetrable. I showed the press this week and they were — they couldn’t believe it. Even the ones who hate me were impressed. You could see it in their faces.

But we’re here. In the tent. Which is — it’s very white. Very clean. Almost as clean as the new bathroom I installed at the White House. Marble and gold. Have you seen it? Some of you have seen it. It’s incredible. People come in and they say, “Sir, this is the most beautiful bathroom in the world.” And I say, “I know.”

Let me tell you about Iran for a second because it’s — Don won’t mind. Don understands priorities. We’re in a very critical stage of negotiations. Can’t go into detail. Classified. But I will say this: when this deal is done — and it’s going to be done, because I always get the deal done — it’s going to be the biggest deal in the history of the Middle East. Bigger than Camp David. Bigger than Abraham Accords, which I also did, by the way, and never got enough credit for. The fake news — and some of them are here tonight, I’m sure, hiding behind the — is that a cake? That’s a big cake. Not as big as my inauguration cake, but big.

Where was I? Iran. Right. So we’re locked and loaded, as I told JD to tell the press, and — JD did a good job at that briefing, by the way. Not as good as me, but nobody is. I do my own briefings better than anyone has ever done briefings. Even my enemies admit that. They say, “Sir, when you brief, it’s like watching a master.” And I say, “I know.”

The ring. Let me see the ring. Hold it up, Don. Hold it up. — Nice. Nice ring. I’ve bought a few rings in my time. I know rings. That’s a — what is that, four carats? Five? It’s fine. It’s a fine ring. For what it is. Melania’s was bigger, but — different situation. Different league, frankly. Melania is Melania. There’s only one. Trust me, I know — I’ve looked.

I want to say something about marriage because I think I have a unique perspective. I’ve been married three times. Some people say that’s a lot. I say it’s experience. I have more experience with marriage than almost anyone. And here’s what I’ll tell you — and this is good advice, Don, so listen — the key to a great marriage is very simple: make sure she knows you’re the most important person in the room. At all times. In every room. Even this one. Even though I’m here, and I’m obviously the most important person in this room — and every room, frankly — within your marriage, within the, uh, the domestic — she should think it’s you. That’s the trick. Let her think it. It’s like negotiating. The best deals are the ones where the other side thinks they won. I wrote about this. It was a bestseller. Many bestsellers, actually. More than almost any author. Some people say more than Shakespeare, but I won’t go that far. I’ll go pretty far, but not that far.

Don Jr. — I’m very proud of you. Mostly. You’ve done some things that — well, we won’t get into that tonight. Tonight is about celebration. Tonight is about family. Tonight is about — [checks phone] — oh, that’s interesting. That’s a very interesting development. I can’t tell you what it is, but it’s big. It’s very big. Iran-related. I’m going to have to —

Let me wrap up. To the bride — you’re a very lucky woman. You’re marrying a Trump. That’s the greatest last name in the history of last names. People would kill for that name. Some people have killed for that name, but that’s — that was January 6th, and they’ve been pardoned, and frankly they’re going to be compensated very soon through a fund that I — but that’s a different speech. That’s a Tuesday speech. This is a Saturday speech.

To Don: I love you. I’m your father. I’ve always been your father, even when you were — even during the difficult — I’ve always been there. In spirit. From a distance. Which, as we discussed, is sometimes necessary for security reasons.

Anyway. Hopefully it’ll be a great marriage. We’ll see. We’ll see what happens.

I have to go. Iran. Very important. Can’t wait. The helicopter is — they’re telling me the helicopter is ready. Marine One. The most beautiful helicopter in the world. It lands on a special pad now, granite, 100 feet, with my seal on it. Because the grass was — but that’s also a different speech.

Cheers. Mazel tov. Whatever you say at these things. It’s going to be a beautiful marriage. Probably. I give it — no, I won’t say that. I won’t put a number on it. Not tonight.

God bless you, God bless the deal with Iran, and God bless the United States of America.

[The president departs. The cake has not been cut. The Secret Service forms a perimeter. The bride, whose name the president did not use at any point in the toast, reaches for the champagne. Don Jr. sits down. The band, which has been instructed to hold, resumes playing. The song is “My Way.”]
