Part Five: Safety Tips from the App

The app reminds all users:

  • If a match tells you on May 18 that they’re leaving their previous partner because the partner “changed,” consider asking what the previous partner would say.

  • If a match describes their last relationship as “the kindness party,” then spends $76 million on door hangers calling people socialists, consider whether kindness was ever the deal-breaker.

  • If a match gives you $158 million, they are not a match. They are a donor. The app does not have a category for this, but our lawyers are working on it.

  • A match who says “it’s different this time” is statistically likely to be correct about the dollar figure and nothing else.
