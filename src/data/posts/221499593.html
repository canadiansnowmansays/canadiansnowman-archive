OFFICE OF RETROACTIVE JUSTIFICATION Division of Electoral Grievance

THREAT REGISTER NO. 2026-1103 Classification: TOP SECRET // NOFORN // NO FACTS Prepared for: The Principal Date: Sunday, November 1, 2026 Days to Election: 2

1. Purpose

On or about October 20, 2026, the Principal concluded, according to three people close to him who spoke to The Atlantic, that Ukraine’s strikes on Russian oil refineries were in part intended to undermine Republican chances in the midterm elections.

The Division was tasked with identifying every other actor engaged in similar interference, so that the Principal’s conclusion could be supported by evidence after it had been reached. This is our standard method.

2. Methodology

The Division began with the finding and worked backwards. Actors were assessed on three criteria: (a) whether their conduct raised fuel prices, embarrassed the Principal, or contradicted him on television; (b) whether they are foreign, or near enough; and (c) whether the Principal has already said so.

Criterion (c) carried the most weight.

3. Register of Hostile Actors

THREAT ACTOR 1: Ukraine Threat level: SEVERE Method: Kinetic interference in the world diesel market, by means of drones launched at the refineries of a country that invaded it. Evidence: Ukraine paused the strikes for three days at the Principal’s request, received nothing in return, and resumed. On Sunday, October 18, it set an oil terminal in St. Petersburg on fire. The Principal has said its president “doesn’t want to end this war, and probably never did.” Aggravating factor: The Ukrainian president called the Principal’s decisions “weak.” The Division has confirmed this word is known to be effective. Mitigating factor: Most of the diesel shortage comes from the war in Iran. See Threat Actor 4, which has been withdrawn.

THREAT ACTOR 2: The Province of Ontario Threat level: PERSISTENT Method: Paid broadcast of a former Republican president speaking. Evidence: In October 2025, Ontario aired an advertisement featuring Ronald Reagan’s 1987 warning against tariffs. The Principal called off trade talks with Canada over it and accused the province of trying to influence a pending Supreme Court case. The Division notes that the hostile material consisted entirely of a Republican icon’s own words, a technique it classifies as “quoting.” Status: File remains open. The Premier has not apologised and, in the Division’s assessment, is not going to.

THREAT ACTOR 3: The Government of Canada Threat level: ELEVATED Method: Politeness, sustained over a long period. Evidence: At Davos in January 2026, Prime Minister Mark Carney described the American-led order as over, without raising his voice. Canada continues to sell New England about 154,000 barrels of diesel a day without asking for sanctions relief, a halt to anyone’s self-defence, or thanks. Assessment: Canada’s refusal to ask for anything is considered the most destabilising behaviour on this register. The Division could not find a way to tariff it.

THREAT ACTOR 4: The Islamic Republic of Iran and the Strait of Hormuz Threat level: CRITICAL WITHDRAWN Method: Closure of the waterway through which much of the world’s oil passes, following the United States’ attack on Iran at the end of February. Evidence: On Saturday, October 17, American gas averaged $4.31 a gallon, up nearly 45 per cent since the war began. Diesel stood at about $6.32. Note: This entry was removed at the request of the Principal’s office, which advised that the war in question was not interference, because it was his.

THREAT ACTOR 5: Senator Darline Graham (R-South Carolina) Threat level: LOW, BUT UNSETTLING Method: Mild disagreement, voiced at a campaign event. Evidence: After the Principal exempted Russia from the sanctions law named for her late brother, the Senator said: “Personally, I would choose to put the pressure on Russia to end the war and get gas prices down. Because I don’t trust Putin, and I don’t trust Russia.” Assessment: The Division regards this as the gentlest act of defiance on file. The Principal is due in South Carolina to campaign for her. Field agents have been told to bring a smile.

THREAT ACTOR 6: Senator Thom Tillis (R-North Carolina) Threat level: RETIRING, THEREFORE UNCONTAINABLE Method: Arithmetic. Evidence: “What part of enforcing a law passed 86-11 by the Senate does the President and his advisors not understand?” He added that the Principal could lower energy prices “by solving the Iran problem, not by doing a solid for Vladimir Putin.” Assessment: The Senator is not running again and has stopped pretending. See also Senator John Cornyn: “Lindsey would be appalled.”

THREAT ACTOR 7: Elon Musk Threat level: UNDETERMINED Method: Contested. Evidence: Mr Musk has spent $366 million on Republican campaigns this year. He has written that any limits on Starshield “are decided by the US military and ultimately POTUS.” The Principal said: “It wasn’t me, and I don’t think it was Elon.” On October 20, the Wall Street Journal reported that it was Elon. Assessment: The Division has listed Mr Musk simultaneously as Donor, Ally, Suspect and Witness. It awaits guidance on which column he will be in by Tuesday.

THREAT ACTOR 8: The Calendar Threat level: IMMINENT Method: Arriving on Tuesday, November 3, regardless of instructions. Assessment: No countermeasure identified.

The Russian Federation Evidence of good faith:

  • Sending, in the Principal’s words, “millions of barrels of diesel to the World” at “a low price.” Analysts put the October promise at roughly 75,000 barrels a day, about half of what Canada sends New England without asking.

  • Fired 39 ballistic missiles at Ukraine overnight into October 14 and struck all three of Kyiv’s heat-and-power plants. Assessed by the Principal on October 19: “Russia, frankly, has been fine on Ukraine. They haven’t been doing much.”

  • Released a 74-year-old American teacher on October 21, after more than four years in a penal colony on charges the State Department called fabricated. The Principal’s envoy thanked its president: “You are a humanitarian also, sir, and I salute that.” The same afternoon, Russian security services arrested a German citizen on similar charges.

  • Proposed, during a birthday telephone call the Principal took against the advice of his aides, a meeting of the great powers in Shenzhen to settle Ukraine’s future without Ukraine.

Assessment: No interference detected. The Division notes that Russia is the only actor on file whose conduct the Principal has thanked it for.

5. Prior Findings

The Division located an earlier assessment of energy prices and elections. It was given under oath at a Senate confirmation hearing in 2025 by the current Secretary of the Treasury, Scott Bessent, who said of the previous administration’s Russia sanctions: “I believe that the previous administration was worried about raising U.S. energy prices during an election season.”

The Division has filed this under Historical Concerns, Since Resolved by Becoming Our Own.
