Sunday Satire. The rewards programme is invented. The $90, the fund it came from, the diesel order and the election date are not.

MAGA REWARDS™
The Loyalty Programme That Remembers You (Until November)

QUARTERLY POINTS STATEMENT
Statement period: 1 July to 30 September 2026
Statement date: 7 October 2026
Member: VALUED SENIOR
Member number: Medicare Part B, as applicable
Tier: Gold (pending results of 3 November)

Congratulations, Valued Senior!

You have been selected to receive a Bonus Reward of $90.00, deposited directly into your bank account in early October 2026. If we do not have your banking details, a cheque is in the post. Please allow time for the cheque to arrive before you vote.

This reward comes to you from the Medicare Improvement Fund, which, as the White House explains in its fact sheet of 2 October 2026, was given $2 billion by Congress to make improvements to the Medicare fee-for-service programme. The fact sheet describes your reward as historic, and notes that the President is the first ever to use this fund to directly lower costs for seniors.

Your programme administrators would add, in the interest of full transparency, that he is also the first to think of it in October of an election year.

Points Summary

Please note: your Bonus Reward covers rather less than half of one month’s Part B premium. It is not intended to cover the premium. It is intended to be noticed.

How Your Reward Was Calculated

Your programme administrators are proud to share the arithmetic.

More than 20 million members are receiving the reward. Twenty million members multiplied by $90 comes to roughly $1.8 billion. The Medicare Improvement Fund held $2 billion. The fund, in other words, has been spent almost in its entirety in a single mailing, on a single occasion, timed to land in letterboxes between the leaves turning and the polls opening.

We regret to inform members that, as a result, the fund will not be available to make any further improvements to Medicare. However, we are confident that you will remember this improvement.

Eligibility: Who Earns Rewards

MAGA Rewards™ is a members-only programme. To protect the exclusive nature of the experience, the following Medicare enrollees are not eligible for the Bonus Reward, according to the fact sheet:

  • Members whose premiums are paid by Medicaid. These are the poorest seniors in the country. They have, we understand, already been rewarded with poverty.

  • Members who pay Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amounts. These are the wealthiest seniors in the country. They have, we understand, already been rewarded with wealth.

Rewards are therefore concentrated in the broad middle of the senior electorate, which is to say the part that turns out to vote in midterm elections and is not yet sure how.

Your administrators wish to stress that this is a coincidence.

Partner Offers

MAGA Rewards™ is pleased to introduce our newest programme partner.

DIESEL REWARDS™
Powered by Executive Order, 5 October 2026

As the White House’s diesel fact sheet of 5 October 2026 explains, truckers and farmers may now use dyed diesel on the highway, with the federal excise tax deferred until the end of the year. The White House estimates savings of over $100 per refill.

Members unfamiliar with dyed diesel should know that it is dyed red for a reason. Untaxed fuel is coloured so that inspectors can spot it in a tank and issue a fine. Under this offer, the fact sheet notes, enforcement may be paused. The red dye remains. The inspector has been asked to admire it.

Fine print for Diesel Rewards™: the tax is described as deferred, not cancelled. Members may be wondering when the deferred tax falls due. Your administrators are pleased to report that the Treasury has been directed to explore ways to eliminate the obligation to pay it. The deferral therefore ends at the end of the year, at which point, if all goes well, it will turn out never to have been owed. The election, by happy chance, falls in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Why am I receiving this in October?
A. October is a lovely month. The leaves are turning, the evenings are drawing in, and on Tuesday 3 November the country holds its midterm elections. These facts are unrelated.

Q. The fact sheet says Democrats raided $20.7 billion from this fund. Is that true?
A. The fact sheet does say so. It refers to the Affordable Care Act as the “Unaffordable Care Act.” Your programme administrators are not in a position to audit a fact sheet that also describes itself as historic. Members wishing to check the history of the Medicare Improvement Fund are encouraged to consult someone other than the person spending it.

Q. Can I redeem my points for anything?
A. Members may redeem their $90 for a little under a fortnight’s Part B coverage, or for a modest grocery shop. Gratitude is redeemable at any polling station.

Q. My approval of the programme has fallen. Will that affect my rewards?
A. Not at all. The programme director’s own approval rating fell to 33 per cent in August, the lowest of his presidency, according to Reuters and Ipsos. Rewards were introduced shortly afterwards. Your administrators believe these events are connected in the way that the weather and an umbrella are connected.

Q. Who do I call with questions?
A. The fact sheet helpfully provides 1-800-MEDICARE for eligibility and the Social Security Administration on 1-800-772-1213 for payment status. Please note that both lines are staffed by public servants paid by the public, and are not affiliated with MAGA Rewards™, which does not exist, and which you have nonetheless already joined.
