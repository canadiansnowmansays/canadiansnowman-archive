Cover Letter

Dear Homeowner,

I am writing to apply for the care of your lawn.

You may know me from my previous position as Speaker of the House of Representatives, second in the line of presidential succession. I held that post from October 2023, and I am leaving it in January for reasons that were explained to me on election night by the voters, whom I had not consulted before.

I believe my experience transfers well. For three years I tended one very large property on behalf of a single owner who did not live on it, did not visit it, and expected it to look exactly as he pictured it at all times. I never once asked him what he wanted done. I knew. And when I did not know, I waited, sometimes for weeks, until it was explained to me on Truth Social.

I bring to your lawn the same fervour I brought to the House. I will not overstep. I will not form opinions about your hedges. Should you be away, I will leave everything exactly as it is until you return, however long that takes and whatever grows in the meantime.

I look forward to receiving your instructions.

Yours in service,
Mike Johnson
