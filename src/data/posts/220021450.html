Intake Form

Case file: 2026-276

Client A: Elon, 55. Occupation: “Yes.”

Client B: The Republican Party, 172. Has been in a number of relationships with powerful men. Describes itself as “a big tent.” Arrived with a fundraising consultant.

Additional party: Donald, 80. Declined to complete the intake form. Took the counsellor’s chair. Remains in it.

Referred by: Client B’s political advisers, who in the spring of 2026 asked Client A to “rebuild the field operation.” The counsellor has asked twice what this means. It appears to mean door-knocking.

Presenting problem: A public separation in June 2025, followed by a period of estrangement, followed by a reunion that both parties describe as “different this time.” Neither party can say what is different, except the amount.

Who is paying for these sessions: Client A. In full. Client A has asked that this not be disclosed. Client A’s own representatives then disclosed it to The New York Times, before Thursday’s filing deadline. The practice does not understand this either.

Session Transcript (Excerpts)

COUNSELLOR: Thank you all for coming. Before we begin, I’d like to establish some ground rules. We use “I” statements. We don’t interrupt. And we don’t post about the session on social media.

ELON: I own the social media.

COUNSELLOR: Then you’ll find it especially easy not to post. Client B, would you like to start?

THE PARTY: I feel hurt when someone I’ve welcomed into the household, given a desk to, given a golden key to, turns round four days later and calls our budget an “abomination.”

ELON: I feel hurt when someone I spent four months cutting spending for passes a bill that adds trillions to the debt.

COUNSELLOR: Good. Those are both “I” statements. Let’s stay with the feeling. Elon, what did you do with that hurt?

ELON: I said he was in the Epstein files.

COUNSELLOR: And how did that land?

ELON: I deleted it.

COUNSELLOR: Deleting a statement is not the same as repairing the harm it caused.

ELON: I also said I regretted some of my posts about the President. That they went too far. That was June 11. In writing.

COUNSELLOR: That’s progress. Donald, would you like to respond?

DONALD: He went CRAZY. I took away his EV mandate and he went CRAZY.

ELON: Such an obvious lie.

COUNSELLOR: Let’s not relitigate June 5 today.

DONALD: I said he could go back to South Africa.

COUNSELLOR: We talked about this, Donald. In this room, we don’t threaten to deport each other.

DONALD: I said “we’ll have to take a look.”

COUNSELLOR: Let’s move on to the other relationship. Elon, in July 2025 you started something with another party.

ELON: The America Party. It was never consummated.

COUNSELLOR: But you announced it.

ELON: I ran a poll. Two to one in favour. Then I didn’t do anything about it.

COUNSELLOR: So an emotional affair.

THE PARTY: It was very upsetting for my Senate candidates.

ELON: It was a poll.

COUNSELLOR: I’d like to talk about family of origin. Elon, before Client B, you were with the Democrats for a long time.

ELON: Decades. Obama, Clinton, Biden. Strongly supported Obama.

COUNSELLOR: And what ended it?

ELON: They became the party of division and hate.

COUNSELLOR: Many clients tell me their previous partner “changed.” It is occasionally true. Was there a particular moment?

ELON: (long pause) August 5, 2021.

COUNSELLOR: What happened on August 5, 2021?

ELON: They had a party on the South Lawn for electric cars and didn’t invite me. I made more electric cars than all of them put together. They invited the union.

COUNSELLOR: And how did you respond at the time?

ELON: I said it seemed odd.

COUNSELLOR: And afterwards?

ELON: I brought it up on Christmas Eve 2023. Also Kamala wrote in her book that it was a big mistake. So I was right.

COUNSELLOR: In this practice, we call that a core wound. It’s interesting that it’s an invitation.

DONALD: I invite him to everything now. Three events in two weeks. Last Thursday I gave him the National Medal of Science. I called him our modern-day Thomas Edison.

COUNSELLOR: And how did that feel, Elon?

ELON: Good. Edison had a lot of patents.

THE PARTY: (quietly) Edison’s film company also filmed an elephant being electrocuted.

COUNSELLOR: Let’s keep it constructive.

COUNSELLOR: I’d like to address the in-laws.

ELON: I called Peter Navarro dumber than a sack of bricks.

COUNSELLOR: Is that an “I” statement?

ELON: I feel that Peter Navarro is dumber than a sack of bricks.

COUNSELLOR: Thank you. And Mr. Bessent?

ELON: We had a disagreement in the hallway.

THE PARTY: It was loud. People heard it through the walls.

COUNSELLOR: Families often fight over money. What was the disagreement about?

ELON: Who got to run the Internal Revenue Service.

COUNSELLOR: (writing) Most families fight about Christmas.

COUNSELLOR: We have a few minutes left. I’d like each of you to name your love language.

DONALD: Praise. Public praise. In front of cameras.

THE PARTY: Money. Ideally $158 million, to the Senate Leadership Fund, in one quarter. More than the other side raised from everyone.

ELON: Federal contracts. Launch licences. Spectrum.

COUNSELLOR: Those aren’t love languages.

ELON: They are where I come from.

COUNSELLOR: Pretoria?

ELON: Starbase.

Counsellor’s Private Notes

Not shared with clients.

The presenting problem is not, in my professional view, the June 2025 separation. Separations are common. The interesting thing is the pattern of the reunion.

Client A leaves when he feels slighted: an invitation not received, a tax credit he couldn’t claim, a bill that ended a subsidy. Client A returns when he feels exposed: when someone in the household mentions the contracts, the subsidies, the launch licences. He then calls the return “love.” Client B calls it “the field operation.” Donald calls it “loyalty,” and has, so far, been the only one in the room unaffected by either feeling.

Client A has gone noticeably quiet on social media this year. Only seven posts about midterm candidates, five about the same Texan. Clients often go quiet after learning that their behaviour has costs. In Client A’s case, the cost was paid in car sales.

Client B appears untroubled that a man who described it as “utterly insane and destructive” fifteen months ago is now its single largest benefactor. Client B has been in relationships like this before. Client B will be in relationships like this again.

Prognosis: Stable until the next bill.

Recommended frequency: Quarterly, timed to Federal Election Commission filing deadlines.
