A WORD FROM THE PROMOTER

Earlier this year we built the most beautiful cage ever built, right on the White House lawn. People said it couldn’t be done. People said it would ruin the grass. The grass is fine. Mostly fine. The cage came down because the cage was too small. Tonight’s cage goes from sea to shining sea, and it’s the biggest cage in the history of cages.

Yesterday the Promoter delivered his closing argument at the Reflecting Pool, which is finished now, and beautiful, American flag blue, the bluest blue. About fifty people attended, which is the right size for a pool. He covered the topsoil, the Bermuda grass, the Corinthian columns and the drainage, and he would like it known that the vandals did it, whatever his own prosecutors say.

The Promoter will not be fighting tonight. The Promoter is standing by.

Mary Peltola (Blue) vs. Dan Sullivan (Red)
The only bout on the card where the Justice Department filed paperwork asking the judges not to announce the result. The judges have so far declined to take the paperwork. The DOJ calls this “election integrity” Ringside calls it “heads we win, tails don’t count.” The red corner has also come prepared: Alaska has two Republicans named Dan Sullivan, the senator and a former mayor of Anchorage, so if one goes down, the trunks are already lettered for the other.
