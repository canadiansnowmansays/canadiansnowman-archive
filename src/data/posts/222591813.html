Sunday Satire. The airline, its tiers and its fine print are invented. Both journeys are real, as described in the note at the end.

WELCOME ABOARD

Thank you for choosing to arrive in the United States of America.

Whether you are a sitting head of state, a defeated presidential candidate, or someone whose passport was cancelled at some point during the trip, we have an arrival experience to suit you. We do not ask what you have done. We ask who you know.

This guide explains our two fare classes, our loyalty programme, and the fine print. Please read it carefully. Your fare class is assigned before you board, and in some cases before you know you are travelling.

SAMPLE ITINERARIES

Itinerary A: Basic Arraignment (January 2026)

Your journey begins at home, in Caracas, at a time chosen by someone else. Transfer is provided by the U.S. armed forces, who will collect you personally. There is no check-in. Your hand luggage consists of your hands, which travel together.

You arrive at a military air base in New York. You are met on the tarmac by federal agents and a large number of cameras. From there you proceed to Brooklyn, where your accommodation has been arranged for an extended stay. You will appear in a Manhattan courtroom to answer charges including narco-terrorism, which you deny. Your return flight has not been scheduled.

Itinerary B: Premium Sanctuary (October 2026)

Your journey begins two days after you lose an election. The day before, your home government asks you to surrender your passports within 24 hours as part of a money-laundering investigation. You post a video promising to comply and assuring everyone you have never considered leaving. On the morning of departure, when the passports have not appeared, the government cancels them. Do not be alarmed. You have more than one passport, and at Premium Sanctuary we find that the number of passports a guest holds matters less than the number of friends.

Board a family van at Foz do Iguaçu and cross the Fraternity Bridge into Argentina at 10:50 p.m. Argentina’s immigration officials will later describe your documents as valid, although Brazil’s federal police will say they have no record of you leaving at all. Forty minutes later, transfer to a private aircraft whose operator has previously flown Argentina’s president. Continue to Guayaquil, Ecuador, then on to Miami.

You will land at a private airport shortly after 9:30 a.m. Please stay seated until the umbrellas arrive. Your S.U.V. is waiting. The State Department has let it be known that visas for you and your family are being prepared.

By the afternoon, you will be ready to address the nation you have just left. Fly America recommends a lengthy social-media statement thanking, by name, the president of the United States and the president of Argentina. Loyalty points are credited on mention.

FLY AMERICA FREQUENT FLATTERER PROGRAMME

Seats are not awarded on merit. They are awarded on status. Here is how to qualify.

Base Member. You have been described by the president as a dictator, a narco-terrorist, or both. You travel Basic Arraignment. Upgrades are not available.

Silver Member. You lost an election the president has described as rigged. You are eligible for complimentary upgrades on request. Proof of the rigging is not required, and is never provided.

Gold Member. You carry a family name the president admires. Umbrella service is included as standard, as is a visa the State Department is already preparing when you call.

Platinum Member. You have made a qualifying contribution. Passport status is irrelevant. Platinum is the only tier with no published entry requirements and no published members. Both are by design.

Programme note: Fly America does not suggest that any particular passenger reached any particular tier by any particular route. We merely publish the tiers. Passengers are welcome to compare notes.

FARE RULES AND FINE PRINT

1. Allegations. Both of our 2026 passengers left their home countries with questions about money hanging over them. One has been indicted. The other is being investigated and denies wrongdoing, calling the inquiry political persecution. Fly America notes this distinction for accuracy. It has no bearing on seat assignment. Seat assignment is based on the passenger, not the money.

2. Passports. Passports cancelled by a government the president dislikes remain valid for travel to the United States. Passports issued by a government the president dislikes may be cancelled by us, together with the government.

3. Elections. If your home country holds an election and you lose, please notify us. If the president has already described it as rigged, there is no need to notify us. We have been expecting you.

4. Concessions. Passengers are not required to concede defeat before boarding. Passengers who do concede, and then explain why they shouldn’t have had to, are treated as not having conceded.

5. Courtesy. Your home country’s president may describe your departure in unflattering terms. Fly America regrets any inconvenience to your home country’s president.

6. Titles. On arrival, passengers may declare themselves the legitimate president-elect of the country they have just left. Should this prove awkward, the declaration may be deleted and reposted with the word “leader” in its place. Our customs form has a box for each. Most passengers declare goods. Premium passengers declare themselves.

CHANGE AND CANCELLATION POLICY

Should your home government ask for you to be returned, the request will be handled by our Customer Relations desk, which also handles requests from Basic Arraignment passengers to be upgraded.

Response times vary. Neither request has yet been granted.

SERVICE ADVISORY

On Friday, 13 November, the Federal Aviation Administration grounded every flight in the country for more than four hours. According to CNBC, more than 10,000 flights were disrupted. The Secretary of Transportation blamed old routers that “got overloaded with data.” Fly America regrets the inconvenience. The customer’s complaint has been logged as a trouble ticket and is being escalated. Passengers are asked to remain seated until the router finishes thinking.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

I am a sitting head of state. Am I eligible for Premium Sanctuary? That depends on your answers to our pre-travel questionnaire, which has one question.

What is the question? Please see the Frequent Flatterer Programme.

I was flown to New York against my will. May I file a complaint? Yes. Complaints are heard in federal court, in Manhattan. Your hearing is scheduled for June 2027.

I fled my country on cancelled passports. May I file a complaint? About what?

Will the president vouch for me personally? He has said that he gave you a pass to enter the country, and that he won’t get involved. Both statements are accurate. Neither cancels the other. Please see Fare Rule 2.

Is there a fare class between these two? Not at present. The middle seat is reserved for countries.

Fly America. Where you land depends on who you know. Whether you land depends on the routers.

Note: This is satire. The airline and its rules are invented. The two journeys, the president’s remarks and the 13 November ground stop are as reported in January, October and November 2026.
