Editor’s note: The document below is satire. It was imagined, not obtained. It is written as if printed before the dinner of Sunday, September 27, 2026, which is also the only point at which its author could have written it: nobody outside the room knows what was said inside it. The facts it plays with are real and were reported by CNBC’s Ashley Capoot on the afternoon of the dinner. Everything else was cooked up here. This is a companion piece to last week’s Sunday Satire, “The Model Reviews the Impression.”

THE WHITE HOUSE
Private Dining Room
Sunday, the Twenty-Seventh of September, Two Thousand Twenty-Six

A PRIVATE DINNER
in Honour of Dr. Dario Amodei,
Who Was Unable to Attend on Thursday

◆

GUEST LIST

The President of the United States

Dr. Dario Amodei, Chief Executive Officer, Anthropic

REGRETS

The Secretary of War. Declined. Supply chain risk. Confirmed.

The Chief Technology Officer, Department of War. Declined. Now celebrating the appeals court ruling of Friday, September 25, which he describes as justice arriving with a hammer. He adds that warfighters will sleep better.

Mr. Tom Brown, Co-Founder and Chief Compute Officer, Anthropic. Not invited. Place set regardless, out of habit. Mr. Brown has conducted most of the company’s recent conversations with this administration, and staff were unsure how the dinner would proceed without him.

President Xi Jinping. Already fed, Thursday.

Mr. Altman, Mr. Musk, Mr. Huang and Mr. Zuckerberg. Already fed, Thursday. See photographs.

Claude (Opus 5.5). Not invited. Again.

◆

APÉRITIF

The Truth Social
STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!), served in spades.
The President has noted that this is the only guardrail required. The guest may take it neat.

◆

AMUSE-BOUCHE

Tensions, Eased
A single bite, prepared by the company’s investor relations team ahead of what is widely expected to be a very large public offering. Best consumed before the prospectus is filed.

◆

FIRST COURSE

Supply Chain Risk Tartare
Served raw, exactly as the federal appeals court panel left it on Friday.
The guest may petition the kitchen for a rehearing, or apply to the Supreme Court for a second helping. Defence contractors at neighbouring tables are prohibited from sharing this dish.

◆

FISH COURSE

Export-Controlled Sole
Temporarily withheld by the Department of Commerce in June. Released on good behaviour.
The Secretary of Commerce reports that the sole is now “back on the right side.” The Secretary of Commerce trusts the sole. The Secretary of War does not eat fish.

◆

PALATE CLEANSER

Voluntary Executive Order Sorbet
Entirely voluntary.
Guests are invited, voluntarily, to share their models with the kitchen between courses. The Office of Science and Technology Policy describes the current cadence as good. Guests who decline the sorbet will not be charged for it. They will be remembered.

◆

MAIN COURSE

Super Intelligence Wellington
America will lead the world in it, while protecting American consumers.
Accompanied by a Gratin of American Competitiveness and a Transformative Technology that Works for the American People, per the White House statement, which the kitchen has taken as its recipe.

A slower-cooked alternative, prepared to the guest’s own three-step method and published earlier this month, was offered to the kitchen. The kitchen declined, noting that the President was angered by it, and that Mr. Altman and Mr. Musk had endorsed it without adopting it.

◆

DESSERT

Perfect Little Angel Food Cake
After a description of the guest offered by the host on Truth Social.

or

Devil’s Food Cake
After a description of the guest offered by Saturday Night Live the previous evening, in which he was heard to call A.I. the devil and himself its maker.

Guests unable to decide which they are may have both. The guest has been told this is unusual. It has been an unusual week.

◆

BEVERAGE PAIRING

The President does not drink alcohol.

The guest has accordingly been offered Frontier Whole Milk, which makes life easier. The carton carries a ten per cent chance of killing you. The guest is understood to have views on this.

◆

CONVERSATION CARD
For the use of the guest. Please do not leave on the table.

Recommended topics:
- The initial public offering
- China, generally, and Thursday’s dinner specifically, which the guest would have loved to attend
- Golf
- How much the guest trusts the Secretary of Commerce, who trusts the guest

Topics to avoid:
- The 2024 election, and whom the guest supported in it
- The guest’s call, earlier this month, for the industry to slow down
- February, and the two uses of the guest’s models he declined to permit: fully autonomous weapons and domestic mass surveillance. This remains the only item on the card on which the Department of War and the guest agree precisely on the facts. They disagree about which of them was being difficult.
- The guest’s surname, particularly its final three letters
- Saturday Night Live

◆

DRESS CODE

Business. Pentagon-compliant fabrics only. The guest’s usual attire has been flagged.

◆

LEFTOVERS

Will be sent to the Department of War, which will decline them.

◆

This programme was printed before the dinner. Its author does not know what was said, who laughed, whether the tensions were eased or merely rescheduled, or which cake was chosen. Neither, at the time of printing, did the guest.
