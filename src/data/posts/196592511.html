FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Office of Monumental Beautification and Aesthetic Dominance Washington, D.C. — The Greatest Capital in the History of Capitals

BRITAIN’S FOREMOST LIVING ARTIST TAPPED TO IMMORTALIZE 45TH AND 47TH PRESIDENT IN BRONZE, GOLD, AND APPROPRIATE HUMILITY

[See Artist’s Approved Rendering, above]

The Office of Monumental Beautification and Aesthetic Dominance is pleased to confirm that the artist’s rendering — unveiled this week to universal acclaim from everyone whose opinion matters — represents the final, approved, non-negotiable vision for what the anonymous British street artist known as “Banksy” clearly intended all along.

A statue of a man holding a flag covering his face, and signed ‘Banksy’, which has appeared in Waterloo Place in London, Thursday, April 30, 2026. Kin Cheung/AP

The original work, installed on a modest grey plinth in Piccadilly, London, was understood immediately by the President’s aesthetic advisors to be a rough draft. The flag was wrong. The suit lacked brilliance. The pedestal said nothing. These deficiencies have now been corrected.

The approved rendering features:

The Flag. Stars and Stripes, full colour, billowing with the precise authority of a nation that has remembered who it belongs to. The original black flag — described by the President as “very gloomy, very anarchist, not a winner’s flag” — has been retired to the Smithsonian’s Department of Foreign Errors.

The Suit. Twenty-four carat. The lapels catch the light in a manner that experts are calling “unprecedented in the history of lapels.” Diamond detailing at the cuffs reflects the President’s well-documented belief that subtlety is a form of weakness.

The Chain. Gold. Substantial. Nodding tastefully to the President’s cultural range and his longstanding relationship with the hip-hop community, which is mutual and tremendous.

The Hair. Unmistakable. Sculpted. Eternal.

The Plinth. THE GREATEST. Two words. The committee considered THE GREATEST EVER but determined that EVER implied there had been others worth comparing. There have not been.

The statue will be reproduced at the following locations, pending appropriations and the cooperation of foreign governments who are strongly encouraged to cooperate:

The People’s Ballroom, Mar-a-Lago — the forty-foot anchor installation, visible from international waters, which the President notes is “basically free advertising for America, which needs it.”

Adjacent to the Trump-America Triumphal Arch, National Mall — where it will serve as human-scale counterpoint to the 250-foot arch, demonstrating simultaneously that the President is relatable and also colossal. Scholars will study this paradox for generations.

The National Museum of the Most Influential Americans in American History — Hall of One, a room designed for a single exhibit, because the curatorial committee felt that sharing wall space with Lincoln or Washington would be, in the President’s precise formulation, “unfair to Lincoln.”

Piccadilly, London — replacing the existing grey version. The British government has not yet agreed to this. Negotiations are ongoing. The President is confident.

When reached for comment, the artist’s representative confirmed that Mr. Banksy had “absolutely no comment, ever, under any circumstances, please stop calling, we are changing our number.” This was interpreted by the Office of Monumental Beautification as enthusiastic acquiescence and possibly a cry for help from a man who has, at last, been understood.

“I always knew that statue was about me,” the President said. “The flag. The walk. The whole thing. Very me. I just made it better.”

He has. The official 24-karat Trump Edition (pictured above) is currently in production.
