Third-place match, sometimes called the Bronze Final now, FIFA’s branding people apparently decided “the game where two heartbroken teams show up because the schedule says so” needed a nicer name.

COLOR: England’s best World Cup finish since 1960, and it happened in the one match this tournament that was marketed, by its own participants, as a formality. Somebody should tell FIFA’s branding department that “Bronze Final” undersells it — this was, goal for goal, a better match than some of the actual quarterfinals.
