A note before the card: The card, its pictograms and its instructions are invented. Everything it describes is real. On September 18, 2026, the White House revoked the hard passes of reporters from CNN, MS NOW and Politico, with the President citing their negativity and lack of truthfulness. On September 24, Judge Timothy Kelly ordered the passes restored, writing that nothing in the record before the lawsuit suggested the revocation was motivated by national security concerns. His temporary restraining order covered hard passes but not pool assignments, a gap described by Latin Times in “CNN Booted From Trump’s Travel Pool Again — Here’s The Legal Gap The White House Is Exploiting”. On September 26, CNN was dropped as the scheduled television pooler for the President’s trip to a college football game in Knoxville, and Real America’s Voice flew instead. On October 1, CNN was dropped again from the Texas and Oklahoma trip. The White House launched its own 24/7 streaming channel, Trump TV: The Essentials Station, on September 21. Saturday Night Live’s Colin Jost joked on October 3 that CNN had been removed midflight. It hadn’t. Yet.

AIR FORCE ONE
PRESS POOL SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS
Rear Cabin · Seats 1 through 13

Please read this card carefully before departure. Your seat may not be here when you return.

Card No. AF1-PP-2026-10 · Revision 14 · Supersedes all previous revisions, including the one issued this morning

Welcome Aboard

Welcome to the rear cabin of Air Force One. You are seated here as a member of the travelling press pool, the small rotating group of reporters who share what they see with every outlet that could not fit on the plane.

Since February 2025, seats in this cabin have been assigned by the White House press office rather than by the White House Correspondents’ Association. The press office thanks the Association for its many decades of service and reminds it that it is no longer needed.

Please note that the rotation remains a rotation. It simply rotates around certain outlets.

1. Before Departure: Confirm Your Seat

Before boarding, all passengers should confirm that their outlet still appears on the manifest. Manifests are updated continuously, including after passengers have packed, travelled to Joint Base Andrews and cleared security.

If you were scheduled to fly as the television pooler and your name no longer appears, do not be alarmed. This is a routine substitution. Your seat has been reassigned to an outlet with a stronger safety record (see Section 7). On some flights, no substitute is assigned and the pool report notes there is “no TV pool.” The cabin crew considers this the safest configuration of all.

2. Your Hard Pass Is Not a Boarding Pass

Passengers holding a White House hard pass restored by court order are welcome to keep it on their person throughout the flight.

Please be advised that the hard pass admits the holder to the White House grounds. It does not admit the holder to the aircraft, the motorcade, the pool rotation, the Oval Office spray or the President’s line of sight. A court order that restores the pass restores the pass. The press office has read the order very closely, and it says nothing about seats.

Passengers with questions about this distinction may direct them to their attorneys, who have described the arrangement as unpredictable and inconsistent. The cabin crew agrees with this description and considers it a feature.

3. Emergency Exits

Emergency exits are located at the front of the aircraft, at the rear of the aircraft, and, for certain outlets, at the gate.

Passengers seated near an exit must be willing and able to assist in the removal of other passengers. If you are not willing, please inform a crew member, who will reassign your seat to someone who is.

In the unlikely event of an in-flight removal, please remain seated until the aircraft has come to a complete stop. The press office has never removed a reporter midflight and would like it noted that this is because it has never needed to.

4. Brace Position

In the event of a follow-up question, assume the brace position:

  • Lean forward.

  • Place your head on the seat in front of you.

  • Do not make eye contact with the President.

  • Remain in this position until the President has finished calling your outlet fake, failing or nasty, or all three.

Passengers are reminded that the brace position is also the recommended posture for reading a Truth Social post about their network.

5. Oxygen Masks

In the event of a loss of cabin pressure, oxygen masks will drop from the panel above your seat. Masks are deployed to approved outlets only. If no mask drops above your seat, your outlet is not approved.

Please secure your own talking points before assisting others.

Pull the mask firmly toward you to start the flow of access. The bag may not inflate. Access is flowing even if the bag does not inflate. If you suspect access is not flowing, please refer to Section 2.

6. In the Event of a Water Landing

This aircraft is equipped with flotation devices for the narrative only.

Reporters who find themselves in the water should remain calm and wait to be rescued by an approved outlet, which will describe the incident as a beautiful landing, maybe the most beautiful landing anyone has ever seen.

7. Unscheduled Substitutions

From time to time, a seat assigned to one outlet will be occupied by another. Recent substitutions include Real America’s Voice, which flew in CNN’s television seat to Knoxville on September 26, 2026 to cover a college football game of considerable national importance.

The press office reminds passengers that substitute outlets have completed the full safety briefing, including the advanced module on asking questions that contain the answer.

8. Reasons for Removal

Removals from the pool may be made for any of the following reasons, which may be selected before or after the removal:

  • negativity

  • a lack of truthfulness

  • national security

Passengers are advised that reasons may be added retroactively. A federal judge has already observed that the national security reason did not appear in the record until after the lawsuit was filed. The press office accepts this observation and regards it as proof that national security concerns can arise at any time, including in court.

9. Prohibited Items

The following items are not permitted in the rear cabin:

  • a second question

  • a printed copy of the President’s previous answer to the same question

  • polling data, unless favourable

  • the word “actually”

  • any network logo that has appeared in a Truth Social post in the past 30 days

Lithium batteries are permitted.

10. In-Flight Entertainment

For your enjoyment, every seat is equipped with Trump TV: The Essentials Station, the White House’s own 24/7 streaming channel, launched on September 21, 2026.

The Essentials Station provides all the coverage the press pool would otherwise provide, without the press pool. Passengers who find this troubling are reminded that they are watching it from the press pool.

Headphones are available on request. Volume cannot be adjusted.

11. Validity of This Card

This card remains valid until October 8, 2026, the date on which Judge Kelly’s temporary restraining order expires. Thereafter, please refer to the next card, which will be issued after the outcome is known and backdated to before it.

Passengers are reminded that this cabin’s seating chart is subject to change without notice, and that “without notice” is itself subject to change without notice.

Thank you for flying with the White House press office. We know you have no choice of carrier, and we intend to keep it that way.
