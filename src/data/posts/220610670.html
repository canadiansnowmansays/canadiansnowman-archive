PART II — BACKGROUND INFORMATION

Purpose of Counseling: Event-oriented. The specific event is the Secretary’s announced plan to livestream a court-martial sentence scheduled for 1300 hrs, 3 December 2026, at Fort Hood, Texas.

Counseling Category: Streaming Discipline.

Summary of Events Leading to Counseling:

On 8 October 2026, the Secretary announced in an interview with Real America’s Voice that the event would be public and streamed online.

Within days, the plan drew opposition from Democrats, Republicans, the Vice President, the retired general who approved the sentence, the Catholic archbishop responsible for every Catholic in uniform, seventy-two retired admirals and generals writing in an open letter, and wounded survivors of the shooting itself.

The Counselor notes this is the broadest coalition assembled by this administration to date. The Secretary assembled it in under a week, without a single tariff.

Army regulations (AR 190-55) prohibit anyone under 18 from witnessing an execution. The livestream plan contained no mechanism to enforce this beyond, it is understood, a checkbox reading “I am 18 or older and/or a warrior.”

PART III — SUMMARY OF COUNSELING

Key Points of Discussion

  1. Prior Content of a Pattern Nature. The Counselor reviewed the Secretary’s existing catalogue: boat strikes in the Caribbean; Iranian positions; a drone-dominance promo scored to “Enter Sandman” until Metallica’s copyright claim had it pulled. The Counselor finds those were fine. The Counselor reposted several of them. The Counselor notes that critics had already raised the problem of treating combat as entertainment, and that the Secretary heard this as a request for a bigger audience.

  1. Audience Analysis. The Secretary told Real America’s Voice the broadcast was necessary because “people need to understand that there’s serious consequences for these types of things.” The Counselor’s finding: people understood perfectly. That is why they objected.

  2. Billing. The Counselor reminded the Secretary that this administration has one stage, and the Counselor is standing on it. As the Counselor understands the office, the presidency is a continuous performance: the rallies, the signing ceremonies with the pen held up to the cameras, the ballroom going up where the East Wing used to be, so that the house finally has a venue worthy of the act. A live broadcast from Texas on a Thursday afternoon with no part written for the Counselor is, in the Counselor’s assessment, not a deterrent but a competing programme. The record should reflect that the broadcast was cancelled on the afternoon the Counselor noticed he was not in it.

In support of this position, the Counselor stated that all the world’s a stage.

This office is obliged to note that the line belongs to Shakespeare, who earned it. Jaques speaks it in As You Like It, and it continues: “And all the men and women merely players.” The Counselor objected to “merely.” Jaques goes on to the seven ages of man. The Counselor read as far as the fifth, the justice “in fair round belly with good capon lin’d,” took it as a personal attack, and asked that it be struck from the text. He did not reach the seventh age, which ends “sans teeth, sans eyes, sans taste, sans everything,” and he has asked that no one bring it up. He did approve of one line, “They have their exits and their entrances,” and asked that the Secretary be shown it.

For the record, the Counselor is no Shakespeare. Shakespeare wrote his own lines.

  1. Stakeholder Feedback (summarised; see enclosures):

a. Enclosure 1. Lt. Gen. Sean B. MacFarland (ret.), who commanded Fort Hood and approved the death sentence, said he was “a hard ‘No’ on livestreaming to the general public because it will become a tool for terrorists and other enemies.” Speaker Mike Johnson, no one’s idea of a dissident, used the phrase “recruiting tool.” The Counselor notes the Secretary removed the Army’s and Air Force’s top uniformed lawyers in his first month, and has not since been troubled by people who explain consequences.

b. Enclosure 2. Archbishop Timothy P. Broglio, of the Archdiocese for the Military Services, issued a statement of disapproval and told Catholic soldiers who might be assigned to the firing squad to request an excusal. The Counselor records this as the first time a chaplaincy has had to issue guidance on a broadcast schedule.

c. Enclosure 3. An open letter from seventy-two retired admirals, generals and military lawyers said the broadcast would turn the punishment “into a carnival for the enjoyment of voyeurs and spectators.” The Counselor notes the Secretary receives a great many letters from retired flag officers and has developed a filing system for them.

d. Enclosure 4. Sen. Jack Reed (D-RI) said the Secretary “deserves to be admonished for his lack of judgment, lack of strategy, and poor communication.” Sen. Thom Tillis (R-NC) called the plan “beyond disgusting,” adding that public executions “are what oppressive dictatorships like Iran and Afghanistan do.” The Counselor notes Sen. Tillis is retiring and is therefore beyond the reach of corrective action.

e. Enclosure 5. See Part III-A. Entered without commentary.

PART III-A — STATEMENT ENTERED WITHOUT COMMENTARY

Staff Sergeant (ret.) Alonzo M. Lunsford Jr. was a combat medic at Fort Hood on 5 November 2009. He was shot seven times, once in the eye. He believes the sentence is just, and he intends to be present on 3 December. “I want to be in the room where I can see him, and he can see me,” he said.

He did not want it broadcast. He said children should not see it, that it should not be “some pay-per-view event, like some John Wick movie,” and that it “shouldn’t be treated like an event you tailgate.”

“As a country, we’re better than that.”

Plan of Action

The Secretary will not stream the event.

The Secretary may continue to stream everything else.

The sentence will proceed as scheduled, witnessed in person by the immediate families of the victims. The Counselor notes this corrective action restores precisely the arrangement that would have existed had the Secretary never had his idea. The Counselor wishes this recorded as a win.

Before announcing future content, the Secretary will read the applicable regulation. The Counselor acknowledges this is a new step and has allowed a reasonable training period.
