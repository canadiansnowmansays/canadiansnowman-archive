Sunday Satire. This memorandum is invented. Pete Hegseth wrote none of it, and no lawyer annotated it. What is real: the departures and their dates, the men named and their records, and the lines quoted from Hegseth’s September 30, 2025 speech at Quantico, where he said every combat position “returns to the highest male standard,” as News4Jax reported, carrying the AP’s account. A follow-up to two earlier Sunday Satires, “Diplomatic Offboarding: Exit Interview, Embassy Athens” and “Welcome Aboard (and Farewell).”

DEPARTMENT OF WAR
OFFICE OF THE SECRETARY

MEMORANDUM FOR: The President
FROM: Secretary of War
DATE: October 17, 2026
SUBJECT: Root-Kause Analysis of Personnel Attrition, Week of October 12, 2026

[OGC: “Cause” has a C, Sir. Counsel raises this only so that no one concludes the K problem is spreading.]

CLASSIFICATION: Unclassified (but please don’t put it in a group chat)

[OGC: The Secretary added the parenthetical himself. Counsel considers it growth.]

  1. Background. Mr. President, in one week the administration lost Kimberly Guilfoyle, Ambassador to Greece, asked to resign on Tuesday, October 13, and Katie Zacharia, Press Secretary, separated on Friday, October 16. You asked whether the losses were connected. Sir, they were. This Department reached its conclusion first and then gathered the evidence.

[OGC: Accurate description of methodology. Not a defence of it.]

  1. Standards. At Quantico on September 30, 2025, I announced that every combat position “returns to the highest male standard.” I said that if no women qualified for some jobs, “so be it.” I said: “This job is life and death. Standards must be met.” Sir, the briefing-room podium is a combat position. So is an embassy. Neither was ever assessed against the highest male standard. That is the root cause.

When her briefing went badly, she cut it short to catch Marine One. When the inspector general’s report went badly, I had my spokesman call it a “TOTAL exoneration” and stayed for the war. When I announced I would livestream a public execution and you overruled me, I stayed for that too. That, sir, is endurance.

[OGC: The Secretary’s endurance is not in dispute. What he endured was largely caused by himself. The inspector general found that he risked compromising sensitive military information. Counsel is aware of no finding that Ms. Zacharia endangered anyone but herself.]

  1. Testosterone. Medical staff declined to explain why male officials survive scandal and female officials do not, so the Department offers its own hypothesis. Testosterone is a natural shock absorber. It allows a man to share attack plans with a magazine editor and feel nothing. It allows an FBI director to send a SWAT team to guard his girlfriend at her performances and feel nothing. It allows a commerce secretary to have lunch on Jeffrey Epstein’s island seven years after cutting him off and feel nothing. Feeling nothing is how you keep your job. Women appear to feel things. Ms. Zacharia told Sean Hannity she “did my very best.” Sir, that is a feeling. We do not do feelings at the Department of War.

[OGC: The hypothesis has no medical support, and the Secretary’s own evidence points the other way. The three men kept their jobs not because they withstood consequences but because none were imposed. The variable is not hormonal. It is presidential.]

[OGC: Counsel also notes that Ms. Guilfoyle denied everything, felt nothing on the record, and was gone in six days. The theory does not survive its own example.]

  1. The Letter K. Sir, my staff identified a second pattern.

Four K’s in one year. In baseball, that can be good or bad, depending on who is pitching and who is batting. A batter who strikes out four times in one game is awarded a Golden Sombrero. This Department does not believe in coincidence. Recommendation: no future appointee should have a first name beginning with K.

[OGC: Counsel accepts the baseball analogy and notes who has been pitching. The women were batting. Counsel, reviewing this memo after Milwaukee won the World Series in five games, adds one point of baseball law: a strikeout is credited to the pitcher. In this administration, it is charged to the batter.]

Sir, my staff also asked why the K’s were hired in the first place. They believe the President simply likes the letter. It reminds him of a better era: the O.K. Corral, thirty seconds of gunfire, and no inspector general. Afterwards, Wyatt Earp was charged with murder, and the judge threw the case out. Sir, that is a TOTAL exoneration. That is the standard I have tried to live up to.

[OGC: Counsel concedes the parallel. Tombstone did not believe it either.]

[Secretary, handwritten: “He remembers another K thing from the old days too. A very klandestine organization.”]

[OGC: Struck. Counsel notes that the Secretary has misspelled “clandestine”, and declines to ask whether it was deliberate.]

[OGC: Ms. Leavitt left voluntarily to be with her two young children and remains one of the President’s outside advisers. She does not belong in this table. When asked to remove her, the Secretary replied that a table with four rows “looks more like a pattern.”]

[OGC: Counsel draws the President’s attention to two further officials whose names begin with K. Kash Patel, Director of the FBI, remains in office despite the drinking allegations, the jet and the SWAT team. Keith Sonderling was promoted into the chair left by Lori Chavez-DeRemer. The letter K is not the operative variable. In this administration, a K followed by a woman has a short tenure, while a K followed by a man is kept on or promoted. Counsel recommends that the Department stop studying the alphabet and start studying the President.]

[Secretary, handwritten: “Kash is different. He’s a fighter.”]

[OGC: So was Ms. Noem. She contradicted the President under oath about an advertising budget. Counsel suspects that was the problem.]

  1. Conclusion. Mr. President, the women did not fail because of anything you did. They failed because they were not built for this. I was. I have been caught more often than all of them put together, and I am still here.

[OGC, final note: Counsel agrees with the Secretary’s last sentence, word for word, and draws the opposite conclusion. That the Secretary has been caught more often than all of them put together and is still here is not evidence that the women were weak. It is the clearest evidence in this memorandum of a double standard.]
