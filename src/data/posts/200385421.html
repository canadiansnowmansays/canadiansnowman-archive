WHEREAS the fund has been withdrawn, extinguished, and pronounced dead, thereby completing the fraud-identification process the President referenced; and

WHEREAS it is therefore confirmed that Vice President Vance did, in fact, catch the fraud; and

WHEREAS the fraud was ours;

NOW, THEREFORE, this Office makes the following findings:

1. The Vice President is to be commended for his diligence.

2. The President is to be commended for bringing the Vice President’s diligence to public attention via social media, including the heroic photograph.

3. No further questions regarding the origins of the fraud, its authorization, or the identity of the individuals who directed its creation shall be entertained by this Office, any federal agency, or the American people, who are asked to focus on the balanced budget, which is coming very soon.

Pursuant to this addendum, the United States hereby:

III. CLARIFICATION REGARDING THE BALANCED BUDGET

The President has stated that if JD’s team finds half the fraud going on in this country — and they will — America will have a balanced budget very soon.

This Office wishes to clarify that the fraud-finding operation, while comprehensive, operates within certain parameters. Specifically:

The operation covers fraud occurring in federal agencies, federal grants, federal programs, federal expenditures, and all activities of the previous administration.

The operation does not cover, and shall not be construed to cover, the financial affairs of the President, his family, his sons, his trusts, his companies, his subsidiaries, or any entity presently known or unknown, all of which are addressed in Section II above and are FOREVER BARRED AND PRECLUDED from examination.

The balanced budget projection has been prepared accordingly.
