Another entry in Stephen’s Sunday Satire series, one that couldn't wait for another Sunday. Nvidia's board brought its magician back this morning, and some acts are best performed while the saw blade and the bunny are still warm. — S.G.

THE SANTA CLARA PALACE OF ILLUSIONS in association with Senior Levels of the United States Government (Uncredited)

PROUDLY PRESENTS

THE GREAT JENSENINI

“Now You See Him, Now You Don’t, Now You Do Again”

A Vanishing in Three Acts Strictly limited engagement: November 12 to November 23, 2026

Running time: eleven days, including interval. Latecomers will be admitted at 9:30 a.m. Eastern, when the doors open and the share price does too.

A NOTE FROM THE MANAGEMENT

Welcome to tonight’s performance.

For thirty-three years, the Great Jensenini has amazed audiences with feats once thought impossible: turning graphics cards into a $5 trillion company, turning a leather jacket into a national symbol, and turning the President of the United States into a speakerphone.

Tonight he attempts his most daring illusion yet. Before your eyes, he will vanish completely from the corner office, remain gone for eleven days, and then reappear in the very same chair, wearing the very same jacket, as though nothing had happened at all.

The management asks that you hold your applause, and your Nvidia shares, until the end.

THE PROGRAMME

ACT I: THE DISAPPEARING CEO Thursday, November 12

The jacket is shown to the audience. It is empty. The cabinet is shown to the audience. It is also empty. The Great Jensenini steps inside. The doors close. A drum roll. The doors open.

He is gone.

In his place stands the company’s chief financial officer, holding a calculator and a set of keys she did not ask for. A statement is read: Mr. Jensenini has departed voluntarily following “personal behavior inconsistent with its code of conduct, standards and cultural norms.” The board accepts unanimously. The audience gasps. Over the following days, some $2 trillion in market value also vanishes, which the management assures you was not part of the act.

ACT II: SAWING THE BOARD IN HALF Tuesday, November 17

A long polished table is wheeled on stage. Seated around it are the directors, early investors and old friends of the Great Jensenini, chosen for their remarkable ability to agree with him.

The chairman rises and tells the audience of Wall Street analysts that there is no plan to bring the magician back.

The saw is produced. The board is cut cleanly in half. Audiences will observe that both halves continue to vote unanimously, in opposite directions, for the rest of the evening.

INTERVAL Wednesday, November 18, to Sunday, November 22

Refreshments are served in the lobby. A petition with 195,000 signatures circulates among the patrons. A gentleman from Texas who builds rockets announces that the magician’s supply contracts were “a handshake with Jensen, not a written contract,” and that without him the whole theatre may collapse.

The Treasury Secretary, who the previous weekend demanded “a dramatic shake-up” and a replacement from outside the company, reappears on Sunday television to explain that the affair was a “canard.” Patrons are reminded that this is not part of the magic act. It is simply what happens to Treasury Secretaries during the interval.

Late on Sunday night, a message appears on the President’s social media account: “I hope the Board of Nvidia rehires Jensen Huang. It would be the best thing for the Company, and also, for the U.S.A.”

The lights dim.

ACT III: THE REAPPEARANCE Monday, November 23, shortly before 9:30 a.m.

The cabinet is wheeled back on stage. The magician’s assistant steps forward to vouch that it contains nothing suspicious. The assistant has been briefed at least twice about what is in the cabinet, and does not recall either briefing.

“Who told me that?” asks the assistant. “I haven’t heard that.”

The doors open. The Great Jensenini steps out, jacket and all, “satisfied with respect to his candor and ability to lead.” The audience is invited to examine the cabinet for themselves. The audience is not permitted to examine the cabinet for themselves.

FINALE: THE TRILLION-DOLLAR RABBIT

The magician reaches into a top hat marked NASDAQ and pulls out a rabbit worth very nearly one trillion dollars. The share price rises by a third before the opening bell has stopped ringing. Members of the audience who bet that the magician would return, at odds of 3 percent at 9:00 a.m., are reminded that the odds were 99 percent by 9:05, and that the management has no idea how that happened either.

NOTES ON TONIGHT’S ILLUSION

Extract from the Minutes of a Special Meeting of the Board of Directors, held Sunday, November 22, at 11:58 p.m., convened following a social media post

Present: All directors. Also present, in an advisory capacity: Senior Levels.

Item 1. Motion to confirm the board’s unanimous decision of November 12 to accept the resignation. Carried unanimously.

Item 2. Motion to confirm the chairman’s statement of November 17 that the board has no plan to reinstate. Carried unanimously.

Item 3. Motion to reverse Items 1 and 2. Carried unanimously.

Item 4. Motion to define “candor.” Tabled.

Item 5. Motion to ask Senior Levels which senior levels they are. Withdrawn.

Item 6. Motion to establish a committee of independent directors to oversee trade compliance and national security risk. Carried unanimously, to general relief that someone, somewhere, would now be independent.

Item 7. The board notes, for the record, that it “received input from senior levels of the United States government” and “is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.” The board notes, also for the record, that the input did not arrive with the intelligence attached, and that nobody thought to ask for the attachment.

Item 8. Any other business: none that the board wishes to know about.

AUDIENCE NOTICES

  • Please do not look at the woman in the yellow dress. The management has been assured by the Assistant that “any woman coming to the country that’s a beautiful woman that comes from China, they say she’s a spy.” The management takes no position on this, except to note that the Assistant’s own intelligence agencies tracked her contacts with China’s Ministry of State Security for more than a year.

  • Flash photography is permitted. Electronic surveillance is also permitted, and was in use throughout the run.

  • The management has received input from senior levels of government and is confident that no national security was harmed during this performance. Patrons who wish to know which senior levels, or what they said, are welcome to write to the box office, which is closed.

  • The Attorney General has confirmed that the government holds no evidence that the magician “has engaged in any unlawful conflict.” The management reminds patrons that finding no evidence of a crime is not the same as finding nothing missing from the cabinet.

  • Defensive briefings were not offered at any point during the run. Patrons concerned that the person beside them may be working for a foreign intelligence service should assume that the government knows, and has decided not to mention it.

  • Polymarket patrons: no refunds. No questions. Especially no questions.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

The Great Jensenini wishes to thank his family for their love and support, the board for its flexibility, the President for his advocacy, and the Nasdaq for its forgiveness. He says he “can’t wait to get back to work.”

The management thanks the audience for its patience and reminds them that the magician will be appearing next at a private House hearing on December 1, and before the Senate Banking Committee on December 3, in a show titled What Did You Know, and When Did You Know It? Tickets are not available to the public.

The Santa Clara Palace of Illusions regrets that the woman in the yellow dress, Megaspeed, the Ministry of State Security and a Shanghai company called Century Litong will not be taking a curtain call.
