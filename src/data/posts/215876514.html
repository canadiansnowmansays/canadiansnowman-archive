A personnel file, not a prosecution — filed on the theory that a man who reviews Supreme Court justices the way other people review a delivery driver deserves his own instalment.

Employee Group: SCOTUS Appointee Division, First-Term Cohort (Class of 2017–2020) Reviewing Manager: D. J. Trump, Chief Executive & Original Hiring Authority Review Period: 20 January 2025 – 15 September 2026 Overall Divisional Rating: DOES NOT MEET EXPECTATIONS Recommended Action: Performance Improvement Plan (attached), tone review, loyalty re-certification

Manager’s Summary Comments

I interviewed these people myself. Personally. In the Residence, sometimes over dinner, which nobody remembers because the press covers other things instead of covering what’s IMPORTANT. I was told, by people whose opinions I valued at the time, that this cohort represented the strongest, most reliable, most conservative bench in the history of the Republic, and possibly in the history of benches.

That is not what I received.

What I received, over the review period above, is a group of senior personnel who have delivered a cascading series of missed targets on core deliverables, several of which were promised to me verbally, in writing, and via extremely positive newspaper coverage at the time of hire. I want to be clear that this review is not personal. It is performance-based, the way all of my reviews are, always, of everybody, and it reflects a Divisional pattern that Human Capital has asked me to document rather than simply discuss on television, which is usually my preferred venue for feedback of this kind.

Below, please find the Divisional Scorecard, followed by individual assessments.

Divisional Scorecard — Eight Matters, Full Review Period

Employee: A. Coney Barrett

Title: Associate Justice Tenure: 5 years, 11 months Hiring Notes on File: “A great, great scholar. Tremendous.”

Key Deliverables Missed This Period:

  • Tariff Authority Initiative. Joined the majority striking down the Chief Executive’s signature emergency tariff programme, a deliverable years in the making, resulting in a refund liability to outside parties running into the tens of billions of dollars — money that, and I want this on the record, we already spent.

  • Birthright Citizenship Directive. Joined the ruling against the Chief Executive’s executive order on constitutional citizenship.

  • Mississippi Late-Ballot Ruling. Not merely joined — authored. Wrote the 5–4 opinion herself, on Company letterhead, without so much as leaving a dissent on file for the Chief Executive to point to later. Zero.

Manager Comment: She clerked for Scalia. I was told this meant something.

Employee: B. M. Kavanaugh

Title: Associate Justice Tenure: 8 years, entire term counted Hiring Notes on File: “Central casting. A fighter. Went through hell for us and he’s tough.”

Key Deliverables Missed This Period:

  • Missouri Congressional Map. Both trips landed on this Employee’s desk as Circuit Justice, and Employee denied relief both times — the second time alongside the rest of the full Court, which at least had the excuse of not being personally assigned to the file.

  • Federal Reserve Personnel Restructuring. Joined the majority blocking removal of a sitting Federal Reserve governor, a personnel decision Human Capital considers well within normal managerial discretion at any organisation of comparable size.

  • Birthright Citizenship Directive. See Barrett, above; Employee was there too.

  • USPS Mail-Screening Stay. Joined the majority blocking the rollout, but filed a separate concurrence explaining, at some length, that the underlying rule was probably lawful and the timing was the only problem. Human Capital is filing this as a partial recovery credit, pending review, on the theory that an employee who explains why he agrees with you right before voting against you is at least showing his work.

Manager Comment: Went through a very hard confirmation. We stood by him one hundred per cent. This is the appreciation we get.

Employee: N. M. Gorsuch

Title: Associate Justice Tenure: 8 years, 9 months (senior-most hire under review) Hiring Notes on File: “A great intellect. Picked him myself, first thing, before anyone else even had a chance to say a name.”

Key Deliverables Missed This Period:

  • Tariff Authority Initiative. See above.

  • USPS Mail-Screening Stay. See above, again.

Manager Comment: I want it on the record that Employee was right there with Alito and Thomas on Cook, on birthright, on the Guard — dissented every time, wanted to rule for us every time. And then tariffs. And then the mail thing. I don’t understand it. I hired him first.
